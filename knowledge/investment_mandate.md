# ARP Global Capital — Investment Mandate (Managed Account)

**Document ID:** POL-INV-001  
**Effective:** 2026-01-01  
**Audience:** Analyst, Risk, Manager, Admin

## Mandate overview

This managed account targets **USD 1,000,000** in assets under management (AUM) for a
boutique discretionary strategy. The book is diversified across US large-cap equities,
core and international equity ETFs, investment-grade and duration fixed income, gold,
a small digital-asset sleeve, and cash.

## Strategic allocation bands

| Sleeve              | Target weight | Hard limit |
|---------------------|---------------|------------|
| US single names     | ~35%          | 45%        |
| Equity ETFs         | ~20%          | 30%        |
| Fixed income        | ~30%          | 40%        |
| Commodities (gold)  | ~7%           | 10%        |
| Digital assets      | ~2.5%         | 5%         |
| Cash                | ~5%           | 15%        |

## Position limits

- No single name may exceed **8% of AUM** at market value.
- Top-three holdings combined must remain below **30% of AUM**.
- Digital-asset sleeve (crypto) must remain below **5% of AUM**.

## Investment process

1. Analyst proposes trade with rationale and sizing.
2. Risk reviews concentration, liquidity, and rule compliance.
3. Trades above **USD 50,000** notional require dual sign-off (PM + Compliance).
4. CIO approval required for new asset classes or mandate exceptions.

## Reporting

Portfolio and risk teams publish daily exposure snapshots. Managers receive executive
summaries only (AUM, P&L direction, headline risk counts) without trade-level detail.
